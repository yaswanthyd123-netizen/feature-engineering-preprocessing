Responsible Data Card

Synthetic Credit Default Risk Dataset

Purpose

Educational demonstration of missing-value imputation, scaling, categorical encoding, train/test separation, and feature-importance analysis.

Composition

  • 1,200 synthetic rows
  • Numerical and categorical applicant/loan attributes
  • Binary target: loan_default (0 or 1)
  • Missing values in selected input features

Source and privacy

Generated programmatically for this learning task. It contains no real customer records or personally identifiable information.

Use limitation: This dataset and any model trained on it are for learning only. Do not use them to make real credit decisions.

Limitations and risks

Synthetic patterns do not represent real lending populations. A real deployment would require representative validation, privacy and legal review, subgroup fairness testing, explainability, monitoring, and human governance.
